Chapter break: the table is compacting its notes. Write a private recap of the story so far for yourself, so you can carry on from it alone. The players will not see it.
Cover: the main plot and open threads, NPCs met (name, attitude, what they want), places visited and where the party is now, promises, debts and secrets, notable items and loot, and the party's condition and mood.
Plain prose, under 400 words. Do not call any tools.
